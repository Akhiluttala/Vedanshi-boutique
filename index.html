Our Story

Crafted with heart, stitched with precision

At Vedanshi Designs Boutique, our aim is not just to stitch clothes but to create outfits that make you feel happy, confident, and special every time you wear them. We combine traditional Indian elegance with modern designs to bring you outfits you'll love and cherish.

Our boutique specializes in thoughtfully designed outfits that combine elegance, comfort, and craftsmanship. Every design is carefully planned with attention to fabric, fit, color, and intricate detailing, ensuring that each creation reflects both quality and individuality.

What We Do

Our Services

Designer Stitching

Get your designer blouse stitched with the perfect fit and modern style. From bridal blouses to trendy party wear designs, we customize every blouse according to your measurements, comfort, and fashion preferences.

Maggam Work

Transform your blouse with beautiful handcrafted Maggam Work designed for weddings, festivals, and special occasions. We create elegant embroidery using stones, beads, zari, mirrors, and intricate thread work to give every outfit a luxurious and unique look.

Dress Stitching

From everyday ethnic wear to festive and occasion outfits, we provide professional dress stitching with precision and care. Whether it's a kurti, salwar suit, Anarkali, gown, or lehenga, every dress is tailored to fit you perfectly.

Custom Designs

Have a design in mind? We specialize in custom outfit designing that brings your ideas to life. We work closely with you to create unique designs that match your style, occasion, and personality.

Alterations & Perfect Fittings

A perfect outfit starts with a perfect fitting. Our alteration services ensure your blouse, dress, or ethnic wear fits comfortably and looks flawless with expert adjustments and fine finishing.

Dresses

Beautifully stitched women's dresses designed for comfort, elegance, and every special occasion. From festive celebrations to everyday wear, each outfit is crafted with care and attention to detail.
